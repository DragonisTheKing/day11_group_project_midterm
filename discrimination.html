No Discrimination

Discrimination is the act, practice, or unfair treatment of a person or group based on a class or categorical basis (such as race, religion, gender, or sexual orientation).

Discrimination at school is very bad. It can lead to:

  • Lower academic engagement
  • Trauma and stress
  • Alienation
  • Higher dropout risk
  • And more...

Unfortunately, discrimination still exists in schools, so if you encounter any forms of discrimination, please find a way to confront or report it, and document the incident if necessary. If you see someone who is lonely or targeted, invite them to join your group or to hang out. If you are being bullied because of your identity, talk to a trusted adult about what to do.
