First, allow us to go over 2 key points when speaking with other members of your community:
1. Remember the Golden Rule we were taught when we were in elementary school: treat others the way you want to be treated.
2. Remember to treat everybody equally, whether your talking to friends, teachers, classmates, etc.
A respectful member of a community should understand that the more respect you project out, the better it is for the entire community (chain reactions!).

Side Note: If someone is not respecting you, please reach out to trusted adults, your parents, or other faculty to make sure you resolve the situation. No one derserves to be disrespected, and the more you advocate for yourself, the more help trusted ones can give you. (the video above goes more into depth about this).
