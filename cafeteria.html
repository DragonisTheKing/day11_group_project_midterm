How should you act towards your fellow lunchmates and staff?

1. Be kind and invite them to sit with you. Ask questions, spread positvity, and encourage one another to collaborate

2. The LPC is a space where we can fill our bellies with delicous food from our amazing staff.

What should you do with your dishes?

In the back of the LPC there is a dish station. You want to throw out any food remains and trash, before placing your forks and plate on separate sides before they are washed by the staff.

How should you check out and pay for your food?

You can either bring your physical card or use the EPS app to scan the qr code linked to your lunch account.

How should you clean up after yourself?

Remove all dishes: plates, forks, cups, trash, etc. Wipe down the area and push your chairs in.
